The Lanzhou rule

One bowl. Five colours.

  1. 一清
    ClearThe broth. Slow-simmered until it runs clear but deep.
  2. 二白
    WhiteThin slices of white radish, softened in the broth.
  3. 三红
    RedChili oil on top. You stir it in, you set the heat.
  4. 四绿
    GreenFresh coriander and garlic shoots.
  5. 五黄
    YellowThe noodles themselves: hand-pulled, glossy and springy.

Pick your width

Same dough. Your width.

In Lanzhou you don’t just order a bowl, you order a width. The noodles are pulled the moment you choose.

二细

Erxi · second-thin, the classic choice

These are the traditional Lanzhou widths. Ask at the counter which ones are on today.
