The house noodle

Biáng! That’s the sound.

Biangbiang noodles come from Northwest China: wide, hand-pulled ribbons of dough. The name is said to be the sound of the dough slapping the counter. Here they are made in-house.

The story

It started as a street stall.

Chef-owner Mike Ng started with a small street-food stall, then brought the concept to Buitenveldertselaan. The food is still street food: quick, fresh and full of flavour.
